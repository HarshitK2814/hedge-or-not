# P6 v2 results: Hedge or Not?, institutional benchmarks and a tail-risk lens

Generated by `scripts/v2/make_results_v2.py` from `results/v2/tables/*`. Pre-registration v2 hash `b2e9a0e506a9` (never edited). Research artifact, not investment advice, not a live-trading instruction.

**Timing.** Ratios are computed at the last business day of month m from data up to and including that day and held from the next business day (monthly rebalance). Returns are monthly USD returns of EFA plus the currency overlay on a nine-currency basket. **Costs.** v1 per-currency trade and roll costs, multiplied by 3 on days after the previous VIX close was above 30; no capacity or market-impact model (an overlay on notional exposure). Sharpe is on total monthly return with no risk-free subtracted unless a table says otherwise. Confidence intervals are stationary-bootstrap 95% (mean block 6 months, 5000 draws). Choices used data up to 2016-12 only; every number dated 2017 or later is labelled `seen period (v1 reported it)`.

## Limitations

- Interbank rate differentials stand in for 1-month forward points; the D6 basis sensitivity is a bound, not a measurement.
- PPP is today's vintage; EFA stands in for the fund's dividend-weighted index.
- The H1 window starts 2006-09, not 2006-01 (first full 60-month estimate); the full-window sensitivity gives the same verdict.
- The literal GJR-GARCH forecast for CHF explodes; H2 is reported literally and with a labelled guard. The lowest vol-cap target on the grid (4%) is chosen in most blocks, so the grid edge often binds.
- No capacity or market-impact model: the overlay is sized on notional exposure, and costs are v1 bp plus the VIX stress multiplier.
- 2017-2026 is a seen period. One USD-carry regime dominates it. Only the forward test can add unseen evidence, and it has 0 months so far.
- Monthly VaR backtests have about 150 observations and little power.
