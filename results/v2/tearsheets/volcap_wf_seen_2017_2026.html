Assumptions

  • seen period (v1 reported it): v1 already reported 2017-2026; descriptive, not out-of-sample evidence.
  • Signals computed at the last business day of month m from data up to that day; hedge held from the next business day (monthly rebalance).
  • Returns: daily EFA (unhedged USD total return) plus the currency overlay on a 9-currency basket, net of costs.
  • Costs: v1 per-currency trade and roll bp, x3 on days after the previous VIX close was above 30 (stress rule). Roll is monthly. No capacity or market-impact model: overlay on notional exposure.
  • Data: Yahoo Finance ETF closes, FRED rates and spot, World Bank PPP; forward points proxied by interbank rate differentials.
  • Book: vol-capped hedge 1 - target / GJR-GARCH vol, target by walk-forward (literal GARCH; the CHF forecast explodes, so CHF is fully hedged at times).
  • Sharpe CIs: stationary bootstrap. Research artifact, not investment advice.
